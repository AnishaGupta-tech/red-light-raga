Why it works
Nobody is punished.
Everybody is in the music.
1

Reward togetherness, not noise

Off-beat hits only make a soft ripple. The music grows when people fall into time.

2

Rooted in everyday India

Raga time theory, the 16-beat Teentaal and the kolam on the doorstep, not decoration.

3

Silence is part of the music

Khali, the empty beat, turns being quiet together into something you do on purpose.

On the street
A permissioned pilot at a busy crossing.
KitWeatherproof projector (about 4–6k lumens, to be confirmed on site), a directional mic or clap sensor, a mini-PC and a low-level speaker.
SafeNo input from drivers, no horns. The wall faces the pavement, away from drivers’ sightlines.
RulesPermissioned pilot. Horn and loudspeaker rules apply, and speaker level stays at ambient.
