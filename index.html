Red Light Raga

रेड लाइट राग

The 90 second red light, turned into a duet between strangers. Clap, hum or tap with the tabla and a kolam draws itself. The raga only blooms when you are in time together.

  1. 01Clap, tap or hum on the beat. Each hit becomes a swara and one more stroke of the kolam.
  2. 02Beat 9 is khali, the empty beat. Stay quiet together and the music grows a layer.
  3. 03When the light turns green, the kolam holds whatever you built.
How will you join in?

A concept prototype by Anisha Gupta. Every sound is synthesised in your browser and the microphone never leaves your device. Without headphones, keep the volume low so the mic does not hear the tabla.
